## Chapter 3 — The Long Watch

Aria stood on the parapet as the light failed. The wind came off the ice fields in long, flat gusts — the kind that found every gap in a cloak.

"You should be inside," said Bram. "The council meets at dawn."

She didn't answer. Below, the [[watchtower]] threw its shadow across the yard, and somewhere beyond it the [[northern-road|northern road]] ran out into the dark.

***
